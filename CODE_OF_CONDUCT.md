# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

Our Pledge

In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming environment, we as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in our project and our community a harassment-free experience for everyone.

Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Showing respect for people’s time and effort

Unacceptable behavior includes: harassment, discrimination, derogatory comments, and other conduct which could reasonably be considered exclusionary.

Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by contacting the project maintainers at [MAINTAINER_EMAIL]. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated and will result in a response that is deemed necessary and appropriate to the circumstances.

Scope

This Code of Conduct applies both within project spaces and in public spaces when an individual is representing the project or its community.
